About

The Photographer

I'm Laila El Zoghbi — a London-based photographer working across editorial, portraiture, documentary, and landscape. My work is drawn to the threshold moments: the minute before a storm, the breath before a laugh, the light at the edge of everything.

Trained at the London College of Communication and under the mentorship of Magnum photographer Ana Esperanza, I've spent the last decade with a camera in countries and cultures that continually rearrange what I thought I knew about seeing.

My work has appeared in Vogue, National Geographic, The Guardian, and Harper's Bazaar. I shoot on both digital and medium-format film — often on the same project.

Services & Pricing

Editorial & Magazine From £1,200 / day
Long-form editorial shoots for magazines, newspapers, and online publications. Includes full-day coverage, post-production editing, and digital delivery within 5 working days. Experience with Vogue, National Geographic, and The Guardian style requirements. Travel within the UK included; international travel by arrangement.
Portrait Sessions From £480 / session
Individual and group portrait sessions for professionals, creatives, families, and artists. Shot on location or in studio. Includes 2-hour session, consultation call, and a curated selection of fully retouched images. Available in both digital and medium-format film.
Brand & Commercial From £2,400 / day
Commercial photography for brands, agencies, and product campaigns. Full creative direction available. Rates include usage licensing for standard brand channels. Multi-day and retainer packages available for ongoing brand partnerships. Previous clients span fashion, hospitality, and lifestyle sectors.
Documentary Projects POA
Long-term documentary and photojournalism projects. Pricing varies by scope, duration, and travel requirements. I work with NGOs, publishers, and independent producers on stories that demand sustained attention and a sensitive eye. Please reach out with a project brief for an initial conversation.
Fine Art Prints From £240 unframed
Limited-edition fine art prints from my personal archive and ongoing projects. Printed on archival Hahnemühle Photo Rag 308gsm paper by specialist labs. Each print is signed, numbered, and comes with a certificate of authenticity. Framing available on request. Editions strictly limited to 25 per image.
Workshops From £320 / participant
Intimate small-group and one-to-one photography workshops covering light, composition, storytelling, and editing. Held in London and internationally at retreat locations. Maximum 8 participants per group. All skill levels welcome. Upcoming dates announced via the mailing list — get in touch to be notified.
